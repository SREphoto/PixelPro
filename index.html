Getting started

1 · Draw your hero

Use the Sprite Editor (or slice a sheet in the Library) and preview the animation live.

2 · Build the world

Generate a tileset, then paint a level with terrain brushes and place spawns, enemies, and coins.

3 · Polish & export

Stylize art in the Lab, then hit Export Game Pack for Godot / Unity / Phaser / Tiled.
